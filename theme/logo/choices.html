The Two Ds

The logo of The Domains of Dread: the Shadow, in the lifted wine red, as you chose. These are the finished files, and below them the mark in place on the trial wiki.

The mark

A roman capital drawn for this mark, not taken from a typeface, with its shadow in wine red. The space between the two letters is cut out, so the mark sits on any dark ground.

With the name

The name is set in Noto Serif Display, a free typeface, and turned into outlines, so the files look the same everywhere.

On the trial wiki

The upgraded copy of your wiki in the Citizen skin with your colours. The mark is at the left of the bar along the bottom. The underlined grey names are links to pages that do not exist yet.

Your colours, as built into the wiki

Black, white and wine red, always dark. Wine red needed two tones: the wine itself is too dark to read as text on black, so links use the same red made lighter.

BlackEvery ground#000000
WhiteHeadings and marks. Running text sits just under it#ffffff
WineButtons and rules. White text on it reads at 9.6 to 1#722f37
Wine for the markThe red of the logo, between the other two: your choice#a12d40
Wine for textLinks on black, at 4.9 to 1. The wine itself would be 2.2 to 1#c95463
