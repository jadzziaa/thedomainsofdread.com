**The Domains of Dread** is a volunteer-run roleplay server for Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition. This page explains what our websites collect about you, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It covers:

- the front page and news at `thedomainsofdread.com`,
- the forum at `forum.thedomainsofdread.com`,
- the public wiki at `wiki.thedomainsofdread.com`,
- the Game Masters' private wiki at `gm.thedomainsofdread.com`.

The sites are run by **[OWNER: who runs the sites]**. You can reach us about anything on this page at **[OWNER: contact address]**.

This policy takes effect on **[OWNER: date this policy takes effect]**.

We do not sell your data. We run no advertising, no analytics and no tracking scripts. We use your data only to run the sites and to keep them safe.

## 1. The front page

The front page and news are plain pages served by **GitHub Pages**. They set no cookies and run no scripts. We do not see who visits.

GitHub, which hosts the pages, records technical details of each visit, such as your IP address, for security and to keep the service running. We cannot see or control those records. GitHub's own privacy statement explains them. **[OWNER: confirm GitHub's log retention]**

## 2. Cloudflare, in front of the forum and the wikis

The forum and both wikis are reached through **Cloudflare**, which protects them from attacks and heavy automated traffic. Every request to these sites passes through Cloudflare first. Cloudflare decrypts the connection on its way to us. This means it handles everything you send to these sites, including your password when you log in and the text of your posts and private messages. It does not keep a copy of pages for later visitors.

Cloudflare may set a cookie to tell people from bots (see section 9). Cloudflare's own privacy policy explains what it records.

The front page is not behind Cloudflare.

## 3. The forum

You can read most of the forum without an account. To post, send private messages or apply for a character, you need a forum account.

**When you register**, the forum stores:

- your username,
- your email address. The forum asks for one when you register. The forum does not send any email at the moment, so the address is not used yet; when mail is turned on, it will be used for account messages such as password resets and notifications you choose,
- your password, stored only as a scrambled hash that we cannot read,
- the date you registered and the IP address you registered from,
- your time zone and language.

You may add more to your profile, such as an avatar, a signature or a location. Only what you add is stored.

**If you log in with Discord**, the forum asks Discord only for basic account details (Discord's "identify" permission). Discord sends your account's ID number and public profile, such as your username. The forum keeps only the ID number. It does not ask for your email address or the servers you are in, and it cannot act on Discord for you. It stores:

- your Discord account's ID number, linked to your forum account,
- a temporary access key from Discord, kept while you are logged in and deleted when you log out or unlink.

Discord learns that you logged in to our forum. You can unlink Discord at any time in your User Control Panel (Profile, then "Manage external account associations"). You still have your forum username and password, so you can always log in without Discord.

**When you use the forum**, it stores:

- your posts and topics, with the date, and the IP address you posted from,
- your private messages, with the IP address you sent them from. Private messages are not encrypted. The people who run the server can technically read the database they are stored in, but we do not read members' private messages. Moderators see a private message only if one of its readers reports it,
- your votes in polls,
- your session: the IP address you connect from, your browser's identification string and the time of your last visit. A session ends after an hour without activity, unless you chose "Remember me",
- failed login attempts, with the IP address and browser they came from, kept for a few hours to slow down password guessing,
- a record of moderator and administrator actions, which can include your IP address when a log entry is about you or something you did.

**IP addresses.** The forum sees your real IP address, passed on by Cloudflare. Staff with moderator rights can see the IP address of a post to deal with spam, ban evasion and abuse. Post and private-message IP addresses are kept **[OWNER: how long post IP addresses are kept]**.

**Avatars.** If you upload an avatar, the forum shrinks it to fit 125 by 125 pixels and stores it. It is shown beside your posts. The forum does not fetch avatars from other sites.

**Character applications.** When you apply for a character, your answers are posted as a topic in a forum that only the staff can see. You cannot see it after sending it, and other members cannot see it. The form asks for your Neverwinter Nights player name, your Discord username and forum name, whether your player name or CD key has changed, details of your character, and that you confirm you are at least 18. Nothing is saved until you send it. Applications are kept **[OWNER: how long applications are kept]**.

**Who sees what.**

| What | Who can see it |
| --- | --- |
| Your username, profile, avatar, signature and public posts | Everyone, including visitors without an account |
| Posts in members-only, faction, staff or GM forums | Only the members of that forum's group |
| Your private messages | You and the people you sent them to; moderators only if a reader reports one |
| Your email address and IP addresses | Administrators; moderators can see the IP address of posts |
| Your applications | The staff |

## 4. The wikis

The public wiki can be read by anyone. Only members of the forum's "Wiki editors" group can edit it. The GM wiki can be read only by the forum's "Game Masters" group.

**Your account.** You log in to a wiki with your forum username and password. The wiki checks them against the forum and does not keep its own copy of your password. The first time you log in, the wiki creates a wiki user with the same name. The wiki may keep a copy of the email address your forum account has, but wiki email is turned off.

**Your edits.** Every edit is kept in the page's history, with your username, the date and time, and your edit summary. Page histories are part of how a wiki works and are kept for as long as the page exists. The wiki also stores the IP address of each edit for a while, for dealing with abuse. It is not shown on the wiki; only the people who run the server can look it up. **[OWNER: confirm how long the wiki keeps editors' IP addresses]**

**Uploads.** Files that editors upload are stored with the uploader's name and the date. On the GM wiki, uploaded files can be fetched only by logged-in GMs.

**If you only read.** Reading a wiki needs no account, and we do not record who reads what.

**Older edits.** Before the wiki moved to its current address, anyone could edit it without an account. Edits made that way show the editor's IP address in the page history instead of a name. If one of those is yours and you want it hidden, ask us (section 7).

## 7. Your rights, and how to use them

You can ask us to:

- **give you a copy** of what we hold about you,
- **correct** something that is wrong. You can change most of your forum profile yourself, in the User Control Panel,
- **delete** your account and data,
- **stop** using your data for a particular purpose, or object to how we use it.

Write to **[OWNER: contact address]** from the email address on your forum account, or send a private message on the forum to an administrator, so that we know the request is yours. We will answer within one month.

**What deletion means on a forum and a wiki.** When we delete a forum account, we choose one of two ways, and we will ask which you prefer:

- **Remove everything**: your account and all your posts are deleted. Replies by others that quoted you remain, and topics you started may lose their first post.
- **Keep the posts, remove the account**: your account, email address, password, profile and Discord link are deleted, and your posts stay, shown under your old name as a guest's posts, so that conversations and roleplay threads still make sense.

Private messages you sent stay in the inboxes of the people you sent them to unless they delete them.

On a wiki, your edits are part of the pages' history and of other people's later work. We can remove your wiki user's details and rename it so that your name no longer appears, and we can hide individual edits. Deleting every edit you made would damage pages others have built on, so we may refuse that part where it would, and we will tell you why.

Some data may stay in backups until they are replaced (section 8). We may keep what we need to deal with a breach of the rules or a legal claim, and will tell you if we do.

If you think we have handled your data wrongly, please tell us first. You can also complain to **[OWNER: data protection authority]**.

## 9. Cookies

We use only the cookies the sites need to work. None is used for advertising or analytics, so we do not ask for cookie consent.

| Cookie | Site | What it is for | How long |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `phpbb3_o4t2q_u`, `phpbb3_o4t2q_k`, `phpbb3_o4t2q_sid` | Forum | Your session: who you are once logged in, and "Remember me". Set for visitors too, so that the forum can tell one visitor from another | One year, or until you log out |
| `phpbb3_o4t2q_track` | Forum | Which topics you have read, for visitors without an account (only if this setting is turned on) | One year |
