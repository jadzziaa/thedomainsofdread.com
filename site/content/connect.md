The Domains of Dread is still being built and is not yet open to players. A full release is intended for early 2027, and it will be announced here and on the forum.

## What you will need

1. **Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition.** The server's own content downloads by itself the first time you join.
2. **A forum account.** [Register on the forum](https://forum.thedomainsofdread.com/), with Discord or with a password.
3. **An approved character.** The server is allowlist-only: every new character is applied for and approved by the staff before it may enter the world. The wiki explains [applications and the allowlist](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Applications_and_the_Allowlist), and the forms are on the forum under Applications.

## What you can do now

- Read the [premise](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Premise) on the wiki.
- Read the [game rules](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Game_Rules) and the [roleplay expectations](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Roleplay_Expectations).
- Make a forum account.
