The Domains of Dread is still being built and is not yet open to players. A full release is intended for early 2027, and it will be announced here and on the forum.

You can get ready now. The server is allowlist-only: every character is applied for and approved by the Game Masters before it may enter the world, and the applications are made on the forum.

## Now, before it opens

1. **Read what the game is.** The wiki's [premise](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Premise), [game rules](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Game_Rules) and [roleplay expectations](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Roleplay_Expectations) say what kind of game this is and what it asks of its players.
2. **Make a forum account.** [Register on the forum](https://forum.thedomainsofdread.com/ucp.php?mode=register) with a username and a password. Once you have an account you can link your Discord account to it and sign in with Discord.
3. **Choose your application.** There are three forms: Normal, Divine and Occult. The wiki's page on [applications and the allowlist](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Applications_and_the_Allowlist) says which one your character needs and what each asks. The answers are long, and the form keeps nothing until it is sent, so write them somewhere of your own first. Write them yourself: an application written or co-written by an AI language model is rejected.
4. **Send it on the forum.** Logged in, choose **Applications** in the forum's menu (or go to [forum.thedomainsofdread.com/apply](https://forum.thedomainsofdread.com/apply)), pick the form, answer every question and send it. Only the staff see it, and you cannot open it again afterward. They reply to you by private message on the forum.

## When it opens

5. **Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition.** The server's own content downloads by itself the first time you join.
6. **Join, and make your character in the game.** A character is let into the world once it is approved and exists in the game's character creation. You may also make it first and apply afterward.

## Finding the server

The server is not running yet, so it is not in the game's server list and there is no address to join. When it opens it will be listed as **The Domains of Dread**, and its direct address will be `nwn.thedomainsofdread.com`. Neither works yet; the day they do will be announced here.
