The Library is a picture gallery of the things that make up the server's world: the furniture of a manor, the stones of a crypt, the carts and lanterns of a village square. Every picture is made from the game's own models, so you see each piece as it looks in the game.

**[Open the Library](https://library.thedomainsofdread.com/)**

## What it shows today

**Placeables**: 2,757 of them, from the team's own work and from community packs on the Neverwinter Vault. Search them by name, or narrow the list by theme (Crypt, Church, Village, Manor and more), by kind (lights, seating, graves and tombs and the like) and by the pack they come from. Open one to see a larger picture, its size in the game, and who made it. Each one has its own link to share.

Not everything the world holds is shown: some of it is kept for the Game Masters, and some waits beyond the Mists.

## What comes next

More sections follow, one at a time: creatures, the people of the world, spell effects, sounds and music, and clothing.

## Credits

Much of what you see was made by builders of the Neverwinter Nights community and shared on the Neverwinter Vault. Every picture names its pack and its author, and the Library's **Source** filter shows each pack's whole contribution in one place. Our thanks to all of them.
