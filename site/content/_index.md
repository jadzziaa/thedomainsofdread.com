---
title: The Domains of Dread
---

A persistent world for Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition, set in Ravenloft as the Advanced Dungeons & Dragons books of the 1990s told it: desolate castles, grey country, and people living their lives in the shadow of the lords who rule them.

The Domains of Dread is a roleplay server. Magic is low to middling and the level cap is 14. Every character is applied for and approved before entering the world, and a death at another character's hand is permanent.

The server is in development. A full release is intended for early 2027; until then the [wiki](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/) holds the setting, the rules and the systems as they are written, and the [forum](https://forum.thedomainsofdread.com/) is open for accounts and discussion.
