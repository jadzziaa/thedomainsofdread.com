## About The Domains of Dread

The Domains of Dread is a roleplay server for Neverwinter Nights: Enhanced Edition, set in Ravenloft as the second-edition books describe it. It is run by volunteers, free of charge, for players aged 18 or older. **[OWNER: who runs the sites]**

## Contact us

Write to **[OWNER: contact address]**. Members of the forum can also send a private message to any administrator.

## Report a post, a page or a member

- **On the forum**, if you have an account: use "Report this post" under the post, or "Report" on a private message. Moderators see every report.
- **Anywhere else**, or without an account: write to **[OWNER: contact address]**. Say which page or post (copy its address), what is wrong and, if you want an answer, how to reach you.

We remove illegal content as soon as we see it. We look at other reports within 7 days. If you report something illegal, you may also report it to the police or the right authority where you are.

## Privacy requests

To get a copy of your data, correct it or have it deleted, see the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and write to **[OWNER: contact address]** from the email address on your forum account. We answer within one month.

## Copyright

If you think something on our sites infringes your copyright, write to **[OWNER: contact address]** with what the work is, where it appears on our sites, your contact details, and a statement that you own the rights or act for the owner. ## Appeals

If we removed something of yours or restricted your account and you think we were wrong, write to **[OWNER: contact address]** within 30 days. A different member of staff will look at it within 14 days.
