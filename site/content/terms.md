## 1. You must be 18 or older

The Domains of Dread is a server for adults. It deals with horror, violence and other mature themes. **You may register, post or play only if you are 18 or older.** By registering you confirm that you are. **[OWNER: how members confirm they are 18]**

If we learn that a member is under 18, we will close the account.

## 2. Your account

- One account per person. Keep your password to yourself.
- You may log in with Discord or with your forum password. You are responsible for what is done with your account.
- Pick a username that follows the rules below. Staff may ask you to change one that does not.

## 3. Rules of conduct and content

The full rules of the server are on the wiki: [Game Rules](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Game_Rules) and [Roleplay Expectations](https://wiki.thedomainsofdread.com/wiki/Roleplay_Expectations). They apply on the forum and the wikis too. In brief:

- **No explicit sexual content.** The Domains of Dread is an 18+ server, and it is not and will never be an erotic roleplay server. Romance and sexual relations may be implied but go no further: every such scene "fades to black". This applies to posts, private messages, images and wiki pages as much as to the game.
- **Some things are never allowed**, not even faded to black: sexual assault; overly detailed and graphic torture, gore, dismemberment or murder; and anything meant to lead into sexual gratification.
- **Nothing illegal.** No content that is illegal where you or we are, and no links to it. This includes, among other things, child sexual abuse material, terrorist content, threats, harassment, hate, fraud and intimate images shared without consent. We remove it and may report it.
- **Respect other people.** No harassment, threats, hate speech or personal attacks, in or out of character. Do not share another person's private information.
- **Keep in character and out of character apart**, as the server's rules describe.
- **No spam**, advertising or automated posting.
- **Respect copyright.** Post only what you have the right to post. Short quotations with credit are fine. Do not post copies of the official Ravenloft books or other published material.
- **No impersonation** of staff or other members.
- **Do not try to break or overload the sites**, get into areas you are not allowed in, or get around a ban.

Horror is the point of the setting. Dark themes are welcome in roleplay within these rules. If a scene makes you uncomfortable, the rules let you step away from it.

## 4. What staff may do

Staff (moderators, Game Masters and administrators) keep the sites running and the rules kept. They may:

- edit, move, lock, hide or remove posts, topics, private messages that are reported, and wiki pages,
- warn members, put their posts into a queue for approval, or suspend or ban them,
- remove a member from groups, including the wiki editors' and Game Masters' groups,
- refuse or close an account.

We will tell you the reason when we remove your content or restrict your account, unless doing so would be unlawful or would put someone at risk. If you think a decision is wrong, you can ask another member of staff to look at it again (see [our About page](/about/)).

A ban on the forum also ends access to the wikis: staff remove the banned member from the wiki groups and block their wiki user at the same time.

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted as the privacy policy explains.
